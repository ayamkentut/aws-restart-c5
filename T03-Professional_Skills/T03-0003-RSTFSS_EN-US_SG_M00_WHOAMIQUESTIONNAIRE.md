## 🗂️ Part 2: Personal Skills Inventory & Translation Table

Use this reference table to translate everyday personal habits into powerful professional language for your resume and interview responses:

### 🗣️ Category 1: How You Think & Communicate

| If You... | You Might Tell An Employer... | Cloud & IT Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Are good at talking on the phone** | *"I communicate well. I’m sociable and enjoy working directly with people."* | Technical Helpdesk, Cloud Customer Support |
| **Are a natural speaker who tells jokes & stories** | *"I’m self-assured and relate well to diverse audiences. I enjoy speaking in public."* | Presenting architecture proposals, team demos |
| **Enjoy numbers and solving math problems** | *"I have strong analytical skills. I am precise, methodical, and detail-oriented."* | Cloud cost optimization, database indexing, metrics |
| **Make, build, or sew things with high craftsmanship** | *"I have excellent manual dexterity, fine attention to detail, and quality control."* | Infrastructure as Code (IaC), precise configurations |
| **Are knowledgeable about music & memorize lyrics easily** | *"I absorb complex information quickly and possess high cognitive retention."* | Learning AWS CLI flags, Linux syntax, networking standards |
